Privacy Policy

Ditdah (ditdah.me and the Ditdah iPhone app) · Effective August 16, 2026

The short version. Ditdah works without an account. Everything you learn is stored on your own device. There are no ads, no trackers following you to other sites, and nothing is ever sold. We count anonymous usage so we can tell whether the app is helping anyone, and we log errors so we find out when it breaks.

What stays on your device

Your progress, settings, scores, badges, streaks, and course position are stored on the device you are using, and by default they never leave it. That covers every mode: Learn, Numbers & symbols, the Koch course, the listening drills, the games, and the cave.

You can erase all of it at any time with Reset progress in Settings. In the iPhone app, deleting the app removes it too.

The iPhone app has no account at all

The app does not offer sign-in, does not ask for your name or email, and has no user profile. To move progress to another device, Settings can show you a progress code that you type in elsewhere. The code encodes your own progress and is not stored on our servers.

The app works fully offline. Every lesson, sound, and image is bundled inside it, so no network connection is required to use any part of Ditdah.

Optional sign-in (website only)

On ditdah.me you may optionally create an account to sync progress between devices, either with Google or with an emailed sign-in link. If you do, we store your email address, an account identifier, and your Ditdah progress with our hosting provider, Supabase. We use it only to sync your progress. Signing in merges your local progress rather than replacing it.

To delete your account and its stored progress, email ryanbyrd@gmail.com and we will remove it.

What we count

WhatWhy
Anonymous visit and page counts To know whether anyone is using Ditdah. Unique visitors are counted from a hash that rotates daily and is never stored as a raw address.
Error reports So we learn when something is broken instead of waiting to be told. Reports carry the error message and where it happened. Email addresses, sign-in tokens and keys are stripped out before the report is sent, and again when it is received.
Learning events, only if you opt in Counts of answers keyed and letters learned, to see which letters are hardest. This is off unless you turn on Tracking consent in Settings.

None of it is used to identify you, to advertise to you, or to follow you across other apps and websites.

Google Analytics (website only)

The ditdah.me website uses Google Analytics for aggregate visit statistics. The iPhone app does not. If you would rather not be counted at all, any content blocker will stop it, and Ditdah keeps working normally.

Camera, for gaze input (website only)

The website has an experimental, off-by-default gaze mode that lets you key Morse by looking left and right. If you switch it on, your browser asks permission and the video is processed entirely on your own device. No image, video, or frame is ever uploaded, recorded, or seen by us. Turning the setting off ends the camera session. The iPhone app does not include this feature and never asks for camera access.

Children

Ditdah is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. There is no account in the app, no chat, no user content, and no advertising.

Who else touches the data

  • Vercel hosts the site and the counters.
  • Supabase stores synced progress for website accounts only.
  • Google Analytics receives website visit statistics only.

We do not sell data, share it with advertisers, or use it for advertising.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect, the effective date above changes with it.
