Support

Dit Dah Me (ditdah.me and the iPhone app) · Updated October 25, 2026

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email ryanbyrd@gmail.com and you will get a reply from a person. There is no ticket system and no bot.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. The iPhone app has no sign-in at all, and everything you learn is stored on your own device.

How do I move my progress to another device?

Settings shows a progress code. Read it on the old device, type it into the new one. Nothing is stored on a server.

The sound does not play

Check the ring/silent switch and the volume. Dit Dah Me plays through the normal media channel, so it should be audible even with the ringer off; if it is not, please email and say which iPhone and iOS version you are on.

How do I erase everything?

Settings → Reset progress. Deleting the app removes it all too.

Accessibility

Dit Dah Me supports VoiceOver throughout, one-switch scanning, and a single-key mode where a short press is a dit and a long press is a dah. If something is hard to use with your setup, please say so in an email; that feedback shapes the app.
